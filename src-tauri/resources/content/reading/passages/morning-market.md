Every Saturday morning, Sari walks to the local market near her house. She likes to buy fresh fruit and vegetables there because they are cheaper and healthier than the food in the supermarket. [[GAP:1]] The market is always full of people, colours, and smells.

First, she visits the fruit sellers. They sell bananas, oranges, papayas, and mangoes from nearby farms. Sari always asks for the price before she buys anything. Sometimes the sellers give her a small discount when she buys a lot. [[GAP:2]] She puts everything into her own cloth bag because she does not want to use plastic.

Next, she walks to the vegetable area. There she buys spinach, carrots, and tomatoes for the week. An old seller often tells her how to cook the vegetables in a healthy way. Sari listens carefully and sometimes writes the recipes in a small notebook.

Finally, she stops at a small stall for breakfast. She orders warm porridge with chicken and drinks fresh orange juice. After eating, she feels ready for the day. She believes that starting the morning with fresh food keeps her body strong and her mind happy.
