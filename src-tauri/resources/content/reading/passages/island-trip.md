title: A Weekend Trip to the Island
level: b1-plus
topic: travel
---

Last month, Dimas and his friends decided to spend a weekend on a small island two hours from the city. They had worked hard for weeks, so all of them wanted a short break from their busy timetables. [[GAP:1]] On Friday evening they packed their luggage and checked the ferry timetable twice.

The journey began early on Saturday morning. The sea was calm, and the sky was clear. During the trip, they sat on the deck, took photos, and planned what to visit first. However, the ferry arrived thirty minutes late because of strong wind near the harbour. [[GAP:2]] Nobody complained, because the view of the island was beautiful.

On the island, they rented bicycles and rode along the coastal road. They stopped at a quiet beach, swam in the clean water, and ate fried fish at a small stall. The stall owner told them which beaches were safe for swimming and where to watch the sunset. In the afternoon, they visited an old lighthouse and learned about the history of the fishermen who lived there. From the top of the tower, they could see the whole coastline and the boats returning to the harbour.

On Sunday, they woke up late and walked through the village market. They bought dried fish and handmade souvenirs for their families. The journey home felt short because everyone was talking and laughing. Dimas said the short trip gave him new energy for work, and they all agreed to travel together again next year.
